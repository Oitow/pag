Welcome to your mixing desk.

Try the two original practice loops first. Start both decks, sync B, then move the crossfader.

  1. Import your own files, or load from the crate with A / B.
  2. Waveform: tap to seek. Lines show the beat grid. BPM detection is an estimate: edit BPM, use Tap, and set “Beat 1 here” to correct it.
  3. SYNC matches the other deck’s tempo and beat phase. Tempo changes pitch too in this version.
  4. CUE sets a return point while paused; while playing it stops and returns there. Hot cues save positions; tap to jump, Shift-click or hold 0.6 seconds to clear.
  5. EQ cuts or boosts low, mid and high frequencies. Start with one deck’s bass lowered to avoid muddy transitions.
  6. Loop repeats 1, 2, 4 or 8 beats from the next grid line. The crossfader blends A and B with an equal-power curve.
  7. Clip exports the selected section as an unprocessed WAV. Record captures the live mixed output, EQ and faders included.

Keyboard: Space = both play/pause, A / B = deck play/pause. Shortcuts are disabled when editing controls. No separate headphone cue or pitch preservation yet.
